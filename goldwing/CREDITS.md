# Honda GoldWing sounds

- **gw_stop, part of gw_idle** - "Inline Six.wav" by dudebrobrodude - https://freesound.org/people/dudebrobrodude/sounds/466715/ - CC0
  (an inline-six running down)
- **gw_low, gw_start, part of gw_idle** - "porsche_911.wav" by mharo - https://freesound.org/people/mharo/sounds/55727/ - CC0
  (flat-six; a steady stretch locked at 1206 rpm, the start cut from the crank and catch)
- **gw_mid, gw_high** - "Porsches.wav" by johntrap - https://freesound.org/people/johntrap/sounds/532108/ - CC0
  (flat-six; steady stretches locked at 2104 / 3560 rpm)
- The loops are OGG (they repeat without a gap), levelled so they don't swell or fade, and the idle (708 rpm, close-mic
  stretches only) and low loops are joined from several stretches (tools/longloop.py) so they don't repeat a short pattern.
- **part of gw_low and gw_idle** - "Porsche.MP3" by mmplisskin - https://freesound.org/people/mmplisskin/sounds/90404/ - CC0
- **shift** - MrM_ElantraGearShfit.wav by mrmccormack - https://freesound.org/people/mrmccormack/sounds/116598/ - CC0
- **grind, road** - synthesised (from the Humvee set)
- **gw_horn** - synthesised twin disc horns (400 + 500 Hz)
