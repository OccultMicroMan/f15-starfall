# Honda GoldWing sounds

- **gw_stop** - "Inline Six.wav" by dudebrobrodude - https://freesound.org/people/dudebrobrodude/sounds/466715/ - CC0
  (an inline-six running down)
- **gw_low, gw_start** - "porsche_911.wav" by mharo - https://freesound.org/people/mharo/sounds/55727/ - CC0
  (flat-six; a steady stretch locked at 1206 rpm, the start cut from the crank and catch)
- **gw_idle, gw_mid, gw_high** - "Porsches.wav" by johntrap - https://freesound.org/people/johntrap/sounds/532108/ - CC0
  (flat-six; steady stretches locked at 708 / 2104 / 3560 rpm)
- The loops are OGG (they repeat without a gap) and levelled so they don't swell or fade on each repeat.
- **shift** - MrM_ElantraGearShfit.wav by mrmccormack - https://freesound.org/people/mrmccormack/sounds/116598/ - CC0
- **grind, road** - synthesised (from the Humvee set)
- **gw_horn** - synthesised twin disc horns (400 + 500 Hz)
